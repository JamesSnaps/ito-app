How to play

{{ engine.MIN_PLAYERS }} to {{ engine.MAX_PLAYERS }} players. Everyone starts with {{ engine.HAND_SIZE }} cards. Be the first to get rid of all of yours.

The cards

Four colours, each with its own animal: the red Fox, the green Frog, the blue Owl and the yellow Duckling. Each colour has the numbers 1 to 9 twice, and two of each action card.

Then there are the wild cards, {{ engine.full_deck()|select("equalto", "W")|list|length }} of each: the Badger and the Bear. {{ engine.full_deck()|length }} cards in all.

Your turn

Put down a card that matches the one on top of the pile: the same colour, or the same number or picture. A ring round the pile shows the colour to play.

Nothing fits? Draw a card. If that card fits, you can put it straight down — or keep it. Otherwise your turn is over.

Action cards

  • Skip (the animal's asleep): the next player misses their go.
  • Reverse: play goes the other way round. With two players, it's a Skip — you go again.
  • Draw two: the next player picks up two and misses their go — unless they put down a Draw two of their own, and then the next player has four to pick up, and so on.
  • Badger (wild): goes on anything. Choose the colour to play next.
  • Bear (wild +4): choose the colour, and the next player picks up four and misses their go. You can only play the Bear when you've no cards of the colour being played. A Bear can't be passed on, and a Draw two can't go on a Bear.

Last card!

When you put down your second-to-last card, tap Last card! — before or as you play it, or any time after while you hold one card. Forget, and anyone can tap Caught! until the next player has gone. If you're caught, you pick up {{ engine.PENALTY }}.

Winning

The first player with no cards left wins. If the draw pile runs out, the pile of played cards (all but the top one) is shuffled to make a new one.

Playing on the app

Play on your own phones (the dealing screen shows a code to scan), pass one device round (hands are hidden while it changes hands), or play against the computer — Easy or Hard. Games against the computer are kept in their own records.
