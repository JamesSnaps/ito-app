Flip 7

How the app scores, and how it plays

Entering a round

Tap the number cards a player got out in front of them, then any modifier cards they played. The running total updates as you go, so you can check it against the table before saving.

How the total is worked out

  • Number cards are added together.
  • x2 doubles that number total — and only that. It does not double the flat modifiers.
  • +2 … +10 are added afterwards, at face value.
  • Seven different numbers is a Flip 7: that player's turn is over — they can take no more cards — and a 15-point bonus is added automatically once the seventh number is tapped. The scorekeeper freezes them for you and moves on to the next player still in.
  • Bust scores zero for the round — modifiers included. Marking a player as busted locks their entry so a stray tap can't give them points, and jumps you to whoever is still playing. A card already tapped can still be untapped, in case it was the tap that was the mistake.
  • Freeze / Bank just locks in what they have. It's recorded the same as any other banked round; the marker is there so you can see at a glance who stopped early.

Ending the game

The first player to reach {{ target }} wins, and the scoreboard calls it. Everyone finishes the round they're in, so enter the whole round before reading the banner.

With a real deck, the scorekeeper only adds up — dealing, hitting and the action cards all happen on the table.

Playing on the app's deck

Play mode is the whole game: the app shuffles the full 94-card deck, deals, and keeps everyone's cards on the table for you.

  • The deal. Everyone gets one card face up, starting on the dealer's left. An action card that turns up is dealt with on the spot, then the deal carries on.
  • Your turn. Hit to take one more card, or Stay to bank what you have. With nothing in front of you, you have to hit. Drawing an action card counts as your hit.
  • Freeze. Whoever draws it picks a player still in — themselves included — who banks their cards and is out of the round.
  • Flip Three. Whoever draws it picks a player still in, who has to take the next three cards. A Freeze or Flip Three drawn during those three waits until all three are out, then gets handed on. A bust or a Flip 7 stops the three early.
  • Second Chance. Keep it: the next time you'd bust, the duplicate and the Second Chance are thrown away instead and you play on. You can only hold one, so a second goes to someone who doesn't have one.
  • Who can be picked. Only players still in the round — not anyone who has stayed, been frozen or bust. The last one in takes the card themselves.
  • The round ends when nobody is left in, or the moment someone flips seven different numbers. Everyone who hasn't bust scores what's in front of them.
  • The deck carries over. Cards from a finished round go to the discards; when the deck runs out, only the discards are shuffled back in. The dealer moves one seat left each round.

On your own phones. The device that dealt shows a QR code under Phones; scan it (or enter the room code), tap your name, and your turns are played from your phone. Anyone who doesn't join is played from the dealer's device, which can also step in for a phone that's gone quiet, or free a seat taken by mistake. When a game ends, New game, same players brings every phone along.

A finished game goes into the records on its own.
