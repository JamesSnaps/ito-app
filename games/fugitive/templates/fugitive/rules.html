How to play

Two players. One is the Fugitive, laying a trail of face-down hideouts from {{ engine.START_CARD }} to {{ engine.ESCAPE_CARD }}. The other is the Marshal, guessing numbers to turn those hideouts over.

The cards

Every card from {{ engine.START_CARD }} to {{ engine.ESCAPE_CARD }} is a hideout number. Each also has footprints (•) — one or two — which matter when it's used to sprint. {{ engine.START_CARD }} starts face up as the first hideout. The rest of {{ engine.DECKS[0][0] }}–{{ engine.DECKS[-1][1] }} sit in three decks: {% for lo, hi in engine.DECKS %}{{ lo }}–{{ hi }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}.

The Fugitive starts with {{ engine.STARTING_CARDS|join(", ") }}, plus {{ engine.STARTING_DRAWS[0] }} cards from the low deck and {{ engine.STARTING_DRAWS[1] }} from the middle one. The Marshal starts with nothing.

The Fugitive's turn

Draw one card from any deck. The Marshal sees which deck, not which card.

Then hide: put a card face down at the end of the trail. It must be higher than your last hideout, and at most {{ engine.MAX_STEP }} higher. To go further, sprint: tuck cards from your hand beneath it, and each one adds its footprints to the distance. The Marshal can see how many cards you sprinted with, but not which.

Or don't hide, and draw one more card from the lowest deck that has any left.

On your very first turn there's no draw: place one or two hideouts.

The Marshal's turn

Draw one card from any deck (two on your first turn). A card in your hand is a number the Fugitive can't be hiding at.

Then guess. Name one number from 1 to 41: if a face-down hideout has it, it's turned over along with the cards sprinted to it. Or name several at once — but they're only turned over if every single one is right.

Winning

The Marshal wins the moment every hideout is face up.

The Fugitive wins by laying {{ engine.ESCAPE_CARD }}, which goes down face up. But if the Marshal hasn't turned over anything above {{ engine.MANHUNT_MAX_REVEALED }}, they may start a Manhunt: guess the hideouts still face down one at a time, carrying on while they're right. Find them all and the Fugitive is caught at the last second; one miss and they're gone. The cards sprinted to {{ engine.ESCAPE_CARD }} are never shown.

Playing here

Each phone that sits down sees only its own player's cards. The table — an iPad or a TV — shows what both players could see across a real table. Passing one device back and forth works too: it hides the hands while it changes hands.

The Marshal's notepad can show what each hideout could still be, worked out only from what the Marshal has seen. Switch it off to keep your own notes.

Which cards carry two footprints is our own spread for now: {{ engine.TWO_FOOTPRINTS|sort|join(", ") }}.
