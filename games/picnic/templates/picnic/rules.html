How to play

{{ engine.MIN_PLAYERS }} to {{ engine.MAX_PLAYERS }} players. Fill your picnic basket with snacks and pack it up to bank the points — but every trap hidden under your blanket springs first. The first to {{ engine.TARGET }} points ends the game, once the round is played out.

The cards

Food scores what's on it: 1, 2 or 3. There are five kinds — sandwiches, strawberries, cupcakes, lemonade and cheese.

Traps go face down under someone else's blanket:

  • Ants carry off your two smallest snacks — into the basket of whoever hid them.
  • Wasp chases off your biggest snack.
  • Seagull snatches your biggest snack for whoever hid it.
  • Dog gobbles half your basket, biggest first.

Defences are played when a trap springs on you:

  • Shoo! sends the trap back: it springs on whoever hid it instead — their Ants bring their snacks to you. They can Shoo! it straight back, and so on, until someone has no Shoo! left.
  • Umbrella stops a trap altogether.

{{ engine.full_deck()|length }} cards in all.

Your turn

Everyone starts with {{ engine.HAND_SIZE }} cards. At the start of your turn you pick up {{ engine.DRAW }}.

  1. Hide a trap, if you like — it doesn't use up your turn. Tap it, then tap a blanket. Not in the first round, only one of yours under any blanket, and a blanket has room for {{ engine.BLANKET }}.
  2. Then lay up to {{ engine.LAY }} snacks in your basket (it holds {{ engine.BASKET }}), or pack up.

Holding more than {{ engine.HAND_LIMIT }} at the end of your turn? Throw some away.

Packing up

Every mystery card under your blanket turns over, one at a time. For each one, play a defence or let it happen. Whatever's left in your basket is banked — safe for good. Snacks the Ants or a Seagull bring you always fit, however full your basket is.

Tip: nobody's hidden anything under your blanket yet? That's a good time to pack up.

Winning

When someone's banked {{ engine.TARGET }}, everyone else gets their turn to finish the round. Then the highest banked score wins — food still in a basket doesn't count. A tie is shared.

Playing on the app

Pass one device round (hands and traps are hidden while it changes hands), play on your own phones (the dealing screen shows a code to scan), or fill seats with the computer — Easy or Hard. Games against the computer are kept in their own records.
